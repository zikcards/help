## Where a refund goes

A merchant refund goes back to the card that was charged, not to your account balance. It arrives once the merchant sends it, and several days is normal. Some merchants take longer.

**Keep the card open until the refund arrives.** If you close a card first, a refund that comes later has to be traced by hand.

## Closing a card

Closing a card stops every future charge on it. On ZikCards, closing is free and returns the remaining balance to your wallet in full, including the small amount the card keeps while it is open.

Before you close a card:

1. **Cancel subscriptions inside each service** that bills the card. Closing the card is not the same as cancelling: the service may keep trying to charge it.
2. **Wait for any expected refund** to arrive on the card.
3. **Then close it** from the card's page.

## Freeze or close?

Freezing pauses a card: every payment is declined until you unfreeze it, and you can unfreeze it at any time. Closing is permanent. Freeze when you want a short pause, close when you no longer need the card.
