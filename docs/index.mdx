---
title: Paying online with a virtual card
description: Practical help for paying online with a virtual Visa or Mastercard, from the ZikCards team.

A virtual card is a real Visa or Mastercard that exists only as a card number, an expiry date and a security code. You use it at an online checkout exactly like a plastic card. Most problems people meet with one have nothing to do with the card itself: they come from a billing address that does not match, a verification step, the card's balance, or money sent on the wrong network.

These pages explain each of those in plain words, with the steps that fix them. They are written by the team behind [ZikCards](https://zikcards.com), which issues US Visa and Mastercard virtual cards funded with USDT or USDC, but the advice applies to most virtual cards.

## The short version

1. **Use the card's own billing address**, not your home address. See [Which billing address to use](/billing-address).
2. **Keep a little more on the card than the price.** Many cards keep a small amount that cannot be spent while the card is open.
3. **If a payment fails, find the reason before you try again.** Retrying the same payment rarely works and can cost a fee. See [When a payment is declined](/declined-payments).
4. **Send stablecoins on the network you chose.** A transfer on the wrong network is usually lost. See [Sending USDT or USDC on the right network](/usdt-networks).
5. **Keep a card open until any refund has arrived.** Refunds go back to the card that was charged. See [Refunds and closing a card](/refunds-and-closing).
