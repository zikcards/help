---
title: Which billing address to use
description: Why a virtual card's billing address matters at checkout, and which address to type.
---

When you pay online, many merchants compare the billing address you type with the address the card issuer has on file. This is called address verification. If the two do not match, the payment can be declined, even when the card has enough money on it.

## Use the card's address, not yours

A virtual card issued in another country usually has its own billing address, held by the issuer. That is the address the merchant's check compares against, so it is the one to type at checkout. Your home address, even if it is correct for you, will not match.

On ZikCards, the billing address is shown on the card's page with a copy button when the card has one. Copy it field by field: street, city, state or region, and postal code.

## Common mistakes

- **Typing your home address.** The most common cause of a declined first payment with a new card.
- **Shortening or changing it.** Even a small difference, such as a missing word or a misspelled street, can fail the check.
- **Leaving the postal code out.** Some checkouts ask only for the postal code, and that alone decides the check.
- **An old address saved in your account.** If a service saved your card earlier with a different address, update the payment method rather than adding the card again.

## Does the name matter?

Most checkouts ask for the cardholder name. Use the name shown on the card page. Merchants rarely check it as strictly as the address, but a matching name avoids a needless problem.
