---
title: When a payment is declined
description: The usual reasons a virtual card payment is declined, and what to do about each before trying again.
---

A declined payment has a reason, and the same details will get the same answer. Find the reason first, change what needs changing, then try once.

## The usual reasons

| What happened | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Not enough money on the card | Load the price plus any tax, plus the small amount the card keeps while it is open. |
| Billing address does not match | Copy the card's own billing address. See [Which billing address to use](/billing-address). |
| A verification step was not finished | Complete the one-time code step. See [Verification codes at checkout](/verification-codes). |
| The merchant's category is not allowed on the card | Some cards block categories such as gambling or cash-like payments. A different payment method is needed. |
| The merchant refuses the card for its own reasons | Merchants run their own risk checks. No card provider can promise acceptance. |
| The card is frozen | Unfreeze it, or use another card. |

## Why not keep retrying

Payment systems read many failures in a row as a warning sign, and some merchants block further tries for a while. Some cards also charge a fee when the same purchase is declined again and again. Stop after one or two tries, check the table above, and only then try again.

## Check what the merchant sees

A merchant sees a card's issuer, type and country from its first digits, called the BIN. The free [BIN lookup](https://zikcards.com/tools/bin-lookup) shows the same information, which helps when a merchant refuses cards from certain countries.
