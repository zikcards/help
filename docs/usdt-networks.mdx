---
title: Sending USDT or USDC on the right network
description: Why the network matters when you send stablecoins, and how to avoid losing a transfer.
---

USDT and USDC are stablecoins that track the US dollar. Each one exists on several blockchains, called networks. When you send them, the network you send on must be the network the receiving address expects. A transfer sent on the wrong network usually cannot be recovered.

## Networks ZikCards accepts

| Coin | Networks |
| --- | --- |
| USDT | BNB Smart Chain, Polygon, Solana, TRON, Ethereum |
| USDC | BNB Smart Chain, Polygon, Solana |

Fees differ by network: Ethereum is usually the most expensive to send on, and BNB Smart Chain, Polygon, Solana and TRON are usually cheaper.

## Before you send

1. **Choose the network first** on the deposit page, then copy the address it shows for that network.
2. **Pick the same network** in your exchange or wallet when you withdraw. The names differ between apps (for example "BEP20" for BNB Smart Chain, "TRC20" for TRON), so read them carefully.
3. **Check the address** before you confirm. The free [USDT address checker](https://zikcards.com/tools/usdt-address-checker) tells you which network an address belongs to and catches a mistyped character.
4. **Send the amount the deposit page asks for,** after the exchange's own withdrawal fee.
